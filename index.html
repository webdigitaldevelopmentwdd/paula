About Eclesare

Crafting Extraordinary Weddings Across Cultures

At Eclesare by Paula, we believe every wedding tells a unique story — a beautiful blend of emotions, traditions and personal dreams. With a passion for Indian and European celebrations, we create bespoke weddings in France, Spain, India and destinations across the world.

From royal palaces and romantic French châteaux to Mediterranean coastal celebrations, we bring cultures together through thoughtful planning, elegant design and personal attention.

From venue selection and décor to guest travel, hospitality, ceremonies and on-the-day coordination, we manage the details so every couple can enjoy their celebration.
